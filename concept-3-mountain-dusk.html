Design Concept 03 — Mountain Dusk: Rationale & Specifications

Concept Direction

Bold, cinematic, and dark-luxury. Inspired by high-end wilderness lodges, film title sequences, and premium outdoor brands. The dark palette creates drama and depth — the forest at dusk. Warm gold accents add warmth and a sense of the sacred. Feels aspirational and immersive.

Color Palette

Night · Forest · Fern · Moss · Warm Gold · Cream. Deep forest greens with gold accents — dramatic, luxurious, and nature-rooted.

Typography

Playfair Display for headings — high contrast, dramatic, with beautiful italic cuts. Used bold for impact and italic for warmth. Inter for body — the most legible system sans-serif, excellent on dark backgrounds. The pairing feels editorial and premium.

Key Layout Decisions

Dark hero with dramatic gradient overlay. Stats bar with hover states creates interactivity. Overlapping image stack in about section adds depth. Asymmetric retreat grid (2:1:1) creates visual hierarchy. Full-bleed land section with overlay content. Consistent gold CTAs throughout.
