Design Concept 01 — Ancient Grove: Rationale & Specifications

Concept Direction

Organic, editorial, and deeply grounded. Inspired by luxury nature lodges and literary journals. The design feels like a hand-crafted field guide — unhurried, tactile, and reverent of the land. Every choice communicates that this is a place of substance and intention.

Color Palette

Bark · Moss · Sage · Warm Gold · Parchment · Cream. Drawn directly from the Juniper woodland environment.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond (serif) for headings — elegant, literary, and humanist. Italic variants add warmth and movement. Jost (sans-serif) for body and UI — clean, geometric, and legible at small sizes. Together they balance refinement with accessibility.

Key Layout Decisions

Full-bleed hero with editorial text overlay. Stats strip anchors the value proposition immediately. Alternating image/text splits create rhythm. Immersive full-width land section in moss green. Retreat cards use cinematic portrait aspect ratios. Generous whitespace throughout.
