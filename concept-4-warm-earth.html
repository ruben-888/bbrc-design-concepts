About Us

Healing, Creativity, and Community

Big Bear Retreat Center is a restorative space for healing and creativity. We humbly offer a supportive environment to gather in community for movement, stillness and practice — trusting in the power of embodied mindfulness and authentic connection.

Our buildings are carefully tended, calm and comfortable. We cook with foods that nourish. And we are deeply committed to making sure everyone who needs to be here can get here.

Design Concept 04 — Warm Earth: Rationale & Specifications

Concept Direction

Approachable, community-forward, and joyful. Inspired by modern wellness apps, boutique yoga studios, and community-centered brands. The terracotta palette feels warm, inclusive, and grounded. Rounded corners and card-based layouts lower the barrier to entry and invite exploration. Ideal for maximizing accessibility and bookings.

Color Palette

Dark Espresso · Clay · Terracotta · Sage · Sand · Cream. Warm and inviting — terracotta as the hero color with sage for nature and cream for breath.

Typography

Lora for headings — a well-balanced literary serif with beautiful italics that feel warm and personal, not stiff. Nunito Sans for body — rounded, friendly, and highly legible. Together they feel approachable and trustworthy without sacrificing sophistication.

Key Layout Decisions

Rounded hero image with pill-shaped CTAs signals approachability. Floating stat badge adds delight. Card-based retreat grid with hover lift effects. Multi-image collage in about section shows community. Terracotta land section creates bold visual contrast. Testimonials with star ratings build social proof. Rounded host CTA banner.
