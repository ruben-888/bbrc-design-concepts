About

A Space
to Restore

Big Bear Retreat Center is a restorative space for healing and creativity. We humbly offer a supportive environment to gather in community for movement, stillness and practice, trusting in the power of embodied mindfulness and authentic connection.

Our buildings are carefully tended, calm and comfortable. We cook with foods that nourish. And we are deeply committed to accessibility — bringing careful attention to our fees so we can do everything possible to get you here.

The Land

1,400 acres of ancient Juniper woodland, originally tended by the Yuhaviatam ("People of the Pines").

Sliding-scale fees and generous scholarships. We honor where you are in your life journey.

Nourishment

Whole, seasonal, lovingly prepared meals to support your practice and presence.

Community

A gathering place for movement, stillness, creativity, and authentic human connection.

Design Concept 02 — Still Water: Rationale & Specifications

Concept Direction

Minimal, airy, and contemplative — inspired by Japanese wabi-sabi and Scandinavian hygge. The design breathes. Generous white space, clean grid structure, and restrained color communicate clarity and calm. Feels like a deep exhale. Ideal for a discerning, design-aware audience.

Color Palette

Ink · Deep Warm · Stone · Sage Accent · Linen · Off-White. Near-neutral with a single sage accent for warmth and nature connection.

Typography

DM Serif Display for headings — a modern serif with optical refinement, excellent at large sizes, and beautiful in italic. DM Sans for body — its companion sans-serif, ensuring visual harmony. The pairing feels contemporary yet warm.

Key Layout Decisions

Split-screen hero avoids the clichéd full-bleed photo. Animated marquee adds subtle life. Retreat listings as a clean table/list — scannable and functional. Two-column about section with card grid. Sage-green CTA band for host section. Minimal footer with strong typographic hierarchy.
