Side-by-Side Comparison

Concept Mood & Audience Typography Key Differentiator Best For
01 Ancient Grove Reverent, literary, nature-immersed. Attracts design-aware seekers who value craft and authenticity. Cormorant Garamond + Jost — editorial serif with clean sans Full-bleed hero, editorial image/text splits, parchment tones, stats strip. Feels like a hand-crafted field guide. Positioning BBRC as a premium, artisanal retreat destination with deep roots in the land.
02 Still Water Calm, discerning, minimalist. Attracts practitioners who value clarity and intentional design. DM Serif Display + DM Sans — refined modern pairing Split-screen hero, animated marquee, retreat list view, near-neutral palette with single sage accent. Maximum breathing room. Conveying stillness and professionalism — ideal if BBRC wants a contemporary, editorial feel.
03 Mountain Dusk Aspirational, dramatic, luxury-seeking. Attracts guests who want a transformative, premium experience. Playfair Display + Inter — high-contrast serif with precision sans Dark mode, cinematic overlays, overlapping image stack, gold accents throughout. Feels like a film poster for the wilderness. Differentiating BBRC in a crowded market — most visually striking and memorable of the four.
04 Warm Earth Welcoming, inclusive, community-driven. Attracts a broad audience including first-time retreat-goers. Lora + Nunito Sans — warm literary serif with friendly rounded sans Rounded hero image, pill-shaped CTAs, card grid, testimonials, terracotta palette. Highest approachability and conversion potential. Maximizing bookings and accessibility — best aligned with BBRC's commitment to belonging and community.
